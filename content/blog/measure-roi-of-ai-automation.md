**To measure the ROI of AI automation, record a baseline for one business KPI before launch, track the same KPI after launch, convert the improvement into money or capacity, and compare it with the full cost of building and running the workflow.** If you do not measure the baseline first, you cannot prove the return later.

## Which KPIs should you track?

Pick the one or two that match why you are automating:

| Goal | KPI | Example target |
|---|---|---|
| Free up people | Hours saved per employee per week | 10+ hours |
| Move faster | Turnaround or resolution time | 2× faster |
| Scale without hiring | Workflows or tickets handled per person | 3× more per team |
| Deflect routine work | Share of routine queries handled by AI | 80% |
| Reduce manual effort | Manual processing or data-entry time | 50–65% less |
| Always-on service | Hours of coverage | 24/7 |

The targets above are outcomes Sixth Gear aims for across engagements, including a 4× faster support investigation in customer service and 65% less manual data entry in field operations.

## What is the formula?

**ROI = (value of improvement − total cost) ÷ total cost**

- **Value of improvement**: hours saved × loaded hourly cost, plus revenue from faster turnaround or extra capacity.
- **Total cost**: build cost + model and infrastructure costs + the cost of people reviewing AI output + ongoing optimisation.

## Why do many AI projects fail to show ROI?

- **No baseline**: nobody measured the process before automating it.
- **Vanity metrics**: counting "AI responses generated" instead of business outcomes.
- **Build and forget**: models, data and processes drift, so performance decays without someone running and tuning the workflow.

## How often should you re-measure?

Track the KPI weekly during a pilot and monthly once it is in production. Treat every drop as a signal to tune prompts, rules, integrations or the human review step. That continuous loop is where most of the long-term value comes from.

## Key takeaways

- Choose the KPI before you build and measure the baseline.
- Count the full cost, including human review and ongoing optimisation.
- Keep measuring after launch; ROI compounds when workflows are actively run and improved.
