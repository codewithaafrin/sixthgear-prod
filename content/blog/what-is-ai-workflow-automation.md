**AI workflow automation is the use of AI models, usually large language models connected to your existing tools, to carry out multi-step business processes end to end.** Unlike traditional automation, it can read unstructured inputs such as emails, PDFs, tickets and call notes, make judgement calls within defined rules, and hand off to a person only where review is needed.

## How is AI workflow automation different from traditional automation?

Traditional automation (scripts, RPA, if-this-then-that tools) follows fixed rules on structured data. It breaks when the input changes shape. AI workflow automation adds a model that can understand language and context, so it can handle the messy, variable work that used to need a person: classifying a request, extracting fields from a document, investigating an issue, or drafting a reply or report.

| | Traditional automation | AI workflow automation |
|---|---|---|
| Inputs | Structured, predictable | Unstructured: text, documents, conversations |
| Logic | Fixed rules | Rules plus model reasoning |
| Handles exceptions | Poorly; stops or errors | Routes, asks, or escalates to a human |
| Best for | Repetitive, identical steps | Repetitive work that needs reading and judgement |

## What does an AI workflow actually look like?

A typical workflow has four parts:

1. **Trigger**: a new ticket, form, email, file or scheduled event.
2. **Understanding**: the model reads the input and extracts what matters.
3. **Action**: the workflow updates systems, runs calculations, drafts outputs or reproduces an issue.
4. **Review**: a person approves, corrects or signs off where the stakes are high (human-in-the-loop).

## When is AI workflow automation the right choice?

It fits best when work is:

- **High volume and repetitive**, but needs reading or light judgement.
- **Slowed by hand-offs**, such as copying data between systems or waiting for someone to triage.
- **Measurable**, so you can prove improvement in hours, turnaround or error rate.

It is a poor fit for rare, high-stakes decisions with no clear rules, or where the underlying data is unavailable.

## Key takeaways

- AI workflow automation runs multi-step processes on unstructured inputs, not just fixed rules.
- Keep humans in the loop where judgement or risk is high.
