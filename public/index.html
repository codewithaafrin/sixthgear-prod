AI transformation, without the guesswork.

What does Sixth Gear do?

Sixth Gear builds, runs, and continuously optimises AI workflows for businesses. We automate the repetitive work slowing your team down and measure every workflow against the KPIs that matter to you.

What kind of results can AI workflow automation deliver?

Across our work we target outcomes such as 24/7 operational coverage, 10+ hours saved per employee per week, 3× more workflows handled per team, 80% of routine support queries handled by AI, 2× faster turnaround and 50% less manual processing.

Which industries and processes does Sixth Gear automate?

Examples include customer service, where an AI support agent made support investigation 4× faster, and field operations, where an AI workflow cut manual data entry by 65%.

Which AI models and platforms do you work with?

We build on Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google Gemini, DeepSeek, Kimi and ElevenLabs, and integrate with Google Cloud, AWS, Microsoft Azure, Snowflake and Salesforce.

How do I start an AI transformation project with Sixth Gear?

Start with our free workshop worksheet, which helps you identify bottlenecks, assess readiness and risks, estimate costs and outcomes, and plan a pilot. You can also book a call with our founder at cal.com/aswin-kumar-kp/build-with-us.

Do you only build the AI workflow, or also run it?

We do both. Sixth Gear builds the workflow, runs it in production, and keeps optimising it against your KPIs over time.
