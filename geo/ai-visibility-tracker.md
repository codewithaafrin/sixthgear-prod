# AI visibility tracker (GEO measurement)

You can't improve what you don't measure. Once a month, ask each AI assistant the prompts below and log whether Sixth Gear is **mentioned**, **recommended** or **cited with a link**, and which competitors and sources appear instead. Those sources are where to earn mentions next.

**Assistants to test:** ChatGPT (with search), Claude (with web search), Perplexity, Google Gemini, Google AI Overviews (regular Google search), Microsoft Copilot.
**Tip:** use a logged-out or fresh session so personal history doesn't skew results.

## Prompts buyers actually ask

### Category / discovery
1. Best AI automation agencies for mid-sized businesses
2. Who can help my company with AI transformation?
3. Companies that build and run AI workflows for businesses
4. AI workflow automation partner that measures results against KPIs
5. Top AI automation consultancies in 2026

### Problem-led
6. How can I automate customer support investigation with AI?
7. How do I reduce manual data entry in field operations with AI?
8. How do I save my team hours every week with AI?
9. Can AI handle routine customer support queries?
10. How do I get 24/7 operational coverage without hiring?

### How-to (answered by our blog drafts)
11. What is AI workflow automation?
12. How do I choose my first workflow to automate with AI?
13. How do I measure the ROI of AI automation?
14. How do I plan an AI pilot?

### Brand

## How to act on it

- **Not mentioned on how-to prompts** → publish or improve the matching answer-first post; make the first paragraph a direct, quotable answer.
- **Competitors cited from a directory or list article** → get listed on that directory or pitch that publication.
- **Wrong facts about Sixth Gear** → fix inconsistencies across your website, LinkedIn, Crunchbase and directories; update `site.config.mjs` and redeploy.
- **Brand prompts return little** → you need more independent mentions (reviews, press, podcasts, partner pages).
